# Changelog

All notable changes to this projec, **directly impacting users**, are recorded
here; changes impacting a person or persons who will use the final produced
binary or binaries in any noticable manner.

Most refactoring, documentation, and other similar changes are **expected to
not** be documented *here*.

The format is [Keep a Changelog 1.1.0](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/) and
the version numbers follow [Semantic Versioning 2.0.0](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

**Track changes** under the `## [Unreleased]` section and in a sub-section
accurately descriping it. *For example*, a bug that was fixed without adding
new "meaningful logic" (explained below) should be placed under the `### Fixed`
sub-section.

**Meaningful logic** is any piece of *code* exposing new business logic or
functionality.
